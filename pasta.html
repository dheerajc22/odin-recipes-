Creamy Pasta

Description

Creamy pasta is a simple and delicious dish made with pasta, cream, cheese, and seasonings.

It is quick to prepare and makes a great meal for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Heavy cream
  • Cheese
  • Garlic
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat butter in a pan.
  3. Add garlic and cook for a minute.
  4. Add cream and cheese.
  5. Mix the cooked pasta into the sauce.
  6. Add salt and pepper.
  7. Serve hot.
