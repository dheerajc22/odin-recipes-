Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken biryani is a flavorful and aromatic rice dish made with chicken, spices, and basmati rice.

It is a popular dish that combines tender chicken with delicious spices and perfectly cooked rice.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Basmati rice
  • Onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Yogurt
  • Biryani masala
  • Salt
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Cook the chicken with onions, tomatoes, and spices.
  3. Cook the rice separately until partially done.
  4. Layer the chicken and rice in a pot.
  5. Cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
  6. Serve hot.
