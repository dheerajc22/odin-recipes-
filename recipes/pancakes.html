Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are soft and fluffy breakfast treats that are easy to make.

They can be served with honey, maple syrup, fruits, or butter.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Egg
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Mix flour, sugar, and baking powder.
  2. Add milk and egg.
  3. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  4. Heat a pan and add a little butter.
  5. Pour the batter onto the pan.
  6. Cook both sides until golden brown.
  7. Serve with your favorite toppings.
